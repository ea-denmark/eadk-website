We're a down to earth community interested in whether helping matters, if systemic change is needed to save the world, and how we'd go about doing any of it.

## Events

We host various events, workshops, social meetups and co-working spaces. Everything from a cozy meetup where we debate ethical dilemmas over delicious vegan food, to workshops on how to leverage your career to have the most impact, and co-working hours to apply the theory in a supportive and focused environment.

You can learn much more about EA through our events, so come by and say hi! Details are posted on Facebook and Slack, and sent out via our newsletter.
